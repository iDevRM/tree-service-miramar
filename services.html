Tree Trimming & Pruning

Tree trimming in Miramar, FL isn't just about looks — it's about safety and tree health. Overcrowded canopies catch wind like a sail during South Florida storms. Dead branches can fall on roofs, cars, or people without warning. Regular trimming removes those risks before they become emergencies — which is exactly why homeowners looking for tree trimming near me should schedule a visit before storm season, not after.

Proper pruning also promotes healthy growth. Cutting at the right points — the branch collar — allows the tree to seal off the cut and resist disease. Improper cuts cause rot and weaken the structure over time. Our crews are trained in correct trimming technique for common South Florida tree species.

Service 3

Stump Grinding

After tree removal, the stump left behind is more than an eyesore — it's a tripping hazard, a host for termites and other wood-boring insects, and in South Florida's humid climate, a breeding ground for fungal growth. Stump grinding removes the stump below grade so you can sod over it, plant a new tree, or simply reclaim the space in your yard.

Full Stump Grinding Info →

We use commercial-grade stump grinders that reduce stumps to wood chips in place. The chips can be left as natural mulch or hauled away — your choice. Most stumps, including large Ficus and Oak stumps, take under two hours to grind. We fill the resulting hole and rake the area smooth before leaving.

Average cost: $100–$400 per stump depending on diameter. Bundle stump grinding with tree removal for the best pricing.

Tree Health Assessments

Not every tree that looks sick needs to come down — and not every tree that looks healthy is actually safe. A professional tree health assessment evaluates structural integrity, root health, signs of disease or pest infestation, and proximity risk to your home or neighboring properties.

We give you a straightforward answer: this tree is fine, this tree needs trimming and monitoring, or this tree needs to come down and here's why. No upselling, no alarmism. Just an honest assessment so you can make an informed decision. Assessments are free with any quoted service.

Palm Care

Palm Tree Trimming

Palms need a different approach than shade trees. A palm has a single growing point at the top and no ability to heal a wound, so aggressive cutting does permanent damage that never grows out. We remove dead fronds, seed pods, and coconuts while leaving the green fronds the palm depends on.

That means we do not do the “hurricane cut” crews sell across Broward every May. Stripping green fronds narrows the trunk the palm is growing at that moment and creates the weak point that snaps in high wind — the opposite of storm protection.

Average cost in Miramar: $75–$150 for smaller palms, $150–$400 for tall coconut and royal palms. Multiple palms in one visit cost less per palm.
