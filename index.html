Know the Signs

When Do You Need Tree Service?

Trees in South Florida grow fast — and problems can escalate quickly in a subtropical climate. If you notice any of the following, it's time to call a professional:

  • Dead or dying branches dropping onto your roof, driveway, or yard
  • Leaning trunk that has shifted noticeably, especially after a storm
  • Roots pushing up sidewalks, driveways, or your home's foundation
  • Tree too close to power lines — anything within 10 feet is a hazard
  • Storm damage from a hurricane or high winds leaving split or hanging limbs
  • Tree blocking light or view and affecting your property value
